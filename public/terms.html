These are the plain-language terms for using Sutros.

What Sutros is

Sutros runs read-only checks on public websites and writes up what it finds. It is a helpful starting point, not a guarantee. A clean report does not mean a site is safe, and a finding may occasionally be wrong. Always confirm with your web person before making changes.

Using it responsibly

  • Checkups are limited in number per account and per site. Do not try to work around those limits or use Sutros to overload a site.
  • Do not use a report to harm the site or its owner. Use it to help.
  • Be honest and respectful on the bulletin. Spam, harassment, and misleading offers are removed and accounts that post them are closed.

Public content

Reports and bulletin posts are public. You keep ownership of what you write, and you give Sutros permission to show it publicly on the site.

The “Checked by SUTROS” badge

The badge is a digitally signed statement that a specific checkup happened on a specific date with a specific result. It is not a guarantee of safety, and it can be verified by anyone at the verify link it points to.

No warranty

Sutros is provided as is, free of charge, without warranties of any kind. We are not liable for decisions made based on a report.
