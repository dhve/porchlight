How the checkup works

Check the evidence, understand the limits

wekup samples public pages and selected checks. It shows what it observed, what could not be checked, and how to investigate a finding. Open the chat to ask questions or challenge a conclusion.

1

We look around

We inspect the website’s public pages and visible technology. When a software version can be identified, we check for known problems and show the evidence for a possible match.

Recon · fingerprint stack, versions, headers, SSL
2

wekup checks the evidence

Scripted checks record what happened. AI adds suggestions and explains possible issues. You can ask wekup to take another look when a finding seems wrong.

Orchestrator · LLM plans & prioritizes probes
3

You help improve the checks

Describe what you see. wekup checks supported evidence again and shows a new assessment beside the original. Your responses guide what the AI verifies on later checkups.

Agent + checks · browse, probe, collect evidence

Under the hood

deterministic

Recon & checks. A crawl plus scripted probes across TLS, security headers and CSP, CORS, cookies, exposed files (.env, .git, backups, keys), leaked secrets and source maps, vulnerable JS libraries, form and CSRF hygiene, and a conservative reflected-input (XSS surface) check. Reliable and safe, no model needed.

llm orchestrator

Choosing checks. Uses the initial observations to select and order checks. The report records which checks completed and which could not.

ai agent

AI browsing observations. Opens pages in a browser and records possible issues. These judgments need confirmation and do not affect the grade. Browsing stays read-only.

llm reporter

AI suggestions. Adds suggested explanations and fixes separately from the measured findings. Scripted rules determine the grade. An incomplete checkup is not rated.

Learning from mistakes

Help wekup get the next check right.

Tell wekup what looks wrong, ask why it reached a conclusion, or describe what you see on your own screen. The engine checks supported evidence again and publishes a new assessment as the conversation progresses. No human approval is needed.

Feedback becomes guidance for what the AI should verify on later checkups. Reports show which guidance was used. Notes are not published, but may be sent to the AI provider for analysis. A vote alone does not prove a mistake, retrain the model, or change a saved report.
