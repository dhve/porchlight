How the checkup works

A careful read of your whole site

Sutros checks what your site is built with, decides what matters, and looks carefully for problems. It never changes anything on your site.

1

We look around

We see what your website is built with and how old it is. Out-of-date software is the most common reason small websites run into trouble, so if yours is behind, we'll tell you exactly what to update and why it's worth doing.

Recon · fingerprint stack, versions, headers, SSL
2

We make a checklist

Based on what we found, we decide what's actually worth checking on your site, so you're not buried in things that don't matter.

Orchestrator · LLM plans & prioritizes probes
3

We gently try things

We look through your pages for security risks and broken parts: files that should be private, weak encryption, out-of-date code, forms that don't work. Nothing on your site is changed. You get a clear list of what to fix, what it means for you, and the steps to do it.

Agent + checks · browse, probe, collect evidence

Under the hood

deterministic

Recon & checks. A crawl plus scripted probes across TLS, security headers and CSP, CORS, cookies, exposed files (.env, .git, backups, keys), leaked secrets and source maps, vulnerable JS libraries, form and CSRF hygiene, and a conservative reflected-input (XSS surface) check. Reliable and safe, no model needed.

llm orchestrator

The brain. Reads recon output, decides which checks to run and in what order, and prioritizes by likely impact. Judgment, not raw execution.

ai agent

The explorer. A browser pass that navigates flows like a curious customer would, catching breakage scripts miss. Read-only: no transactions, detection not exploitation.

llm reporter

The writer. Turns structured findings into a plain-language report with an A–F grade, real-world impact, and a simple fix for each issue.
