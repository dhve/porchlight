Privacy

Last updated September 2, 2026

Sutros is a free website checkup for small businesses, community groups, and local governments. This page explains what we collect and why, in plain words.

What a checkup does

When you check a website, Sutros reads the site the way a normal visitor’s browser would: it loads pages that are already public, reads response headers, and tries a small, fixed list of well-known addresses. It never logs in, never submits forms, never changes anything, and never downloads the contents of any private file it finds (it only notes that the file is reachable).

Checkups are public

Every checkup report is public and is kept so the community can see the state of a site and help fix it. Reports include the site address, the grade, the findings, and short technical evidence. They never include the contents of exposed files, secret values, or personal data found on the site; those are redacted.

Pictures of pages and your feedback

To show where a problem is, a checkup may take a picture of the public page it was found on, with the broken part outlined. Pictures show only what any visitor would see, are kept with the report for about two months, and are then deleted. Readers can also say whether a finding was accurate and leave a short note; those notes are public and are used to make later checkups of the same site more accurate. Feedback from people who are not signed in is tied to a one-way code made from the connection address and browser, not to a name.

Your account

If you create an account we store your email address, a name, and, if you sign in with Google or GitHub, the profile id, name, and avatar those services share with us. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash. We keep a session cookie so you stay signed in. We use your email only to verify your account, to reset your password, and for messages you ask for. We do not sell or share your information.

The community bulletin

Anything you post to the bulletin (a checkup, an offer to help, your contact details in an offer) is public. Post only what you are comfortable making public.

Cookies and logs

We use one cookie to keep you signed in and one to remember your preferences. Our server keeps short-term technical logs (addresses and timestamps) to keep the service running and to stop abuse.

Site owners

If your site was checked and you would like a report taken down or your site excluded from future checkups, email us and we will do it.
