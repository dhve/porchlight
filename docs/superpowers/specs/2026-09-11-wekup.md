# Sutros community and wekup

Sutros is the community for people who need help with websites and people who can offer it. wekup is the AI website checkup feature within that community. Preserve the existing green and warm white identity, public bulletin, helpers, nominations and public checkups.

The homepage leads with community participation and introduces wekup in a separate feature card. Use an original popup inspired by Uiverse's rounded cards, soft shadows, subtle gradients and tactile controls. Keep the name lowercase. The popup has an accessible launcher, close control, report/finding context, private conversation, progress, message composer and short suggested questions. On small screens it fills the available width without clipping or covering its own controls. Support keyboard operation and reduced motion.

Signed-in users can discuss a saved report or a particular finding. A natural-language challenge triggers bounded evidence verification and automatic feedback guidance. Display updated assessments in the conversation and beside the affected finding without refreshing the page or rewriting the signed original. Label current observations and AI interpretations precisely. A current success does not establish an earlier error. A user vote alone cannot confirm a finding.

Conversations are private to an account. Public assessments contain only safe observations and fixed explanatory text. Future checkups receive canonical verification reminders, never private chat text. A verified account is required for chat processing and checkups. Anonymous visitors can browse the community, read reports, open the popup explanation and sign in to continue. Outside a saved report the popup explains wekup and offers a checkup or an existing report; it must not simulate a conversation with canned replies.

Backend endpoints and ownership are specified in the shared wekup contract. The backend stores queued turns durably, enforces idempotency and resource limits, and resumes interrupted work. The frontend rejects stale responses, preserves drafts while polling, stops polling on logout/navigation and clears private messages when the account changes. Original evidence, signatures and grades remain available.

Validate with real PostgreSQL, HTTP routes and local Chromium fixtures. Mock only external AI and network boundaries in tests. Release through the established DigitalOcean deployment after review, backup and the combined tests. Preserve existing accounts, reports, feedback and deployment secrets.
